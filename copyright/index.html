5. The Sun Also Rises — Separate VMH Editions

VMH publishes distinct editions of Ernest Hemingway’s The Sun Also Rises. The Sun Also Rises (Annotated) and The Sun Also Rises: A 20th Century Classic are separate VMH products with different edition identities, cover assets, and added editorial matter. The annotated eBook and annotated paperback are also separate format products. Nothing in this notice should be read as merging those editions or their respective covers.
