About Valois Media

Books with character, context, and lasting value.

Valois Media is a publishing brand devoted to original writing and carefully prepared literary editions. Our work brings together authors, editors, illustrators, and readers through books designed for both physical shelves and digital libraries.

Our publishing approach

We value clear presentation, strong editorial framing, distinctive visual identity, and reading experiences that respect the work and the reader. Selected editions may include forewords, original illustration, annotations, bibliographies, or other contextual material.

Digital reading through Lumière

VMH Lumière is the private reader and library experience for eligible digital purchases made through Valois Media. It is designed to make owned titles accessible across supported devices while preserving customer accounts, entitlements, and reading progress.

Operations

Valois Media Holdings is a publishing brand operated by Cook Services Company, LLC, an Indiana limited liability company.
